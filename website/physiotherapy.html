Our approach

Root cause over symptom relief

Are you dealing with pain, restrictions, or simply looking to improve your fitness and mobility? At Framework, we offer personalized sessions led by our experienced physiotherapists.

With a comprehensive medical assessment, our goal is to identify the root cause of your issues rather than simply treating the symptoms. We combine manual therapy, movement re-education, and exercise prescription tailored specifically to your needs.

Whether you're recovering from an injury, managing a chronic condition, or optimizing performance — we work with you at every stage.

Medical Assessment

A thorough initial evaluation to understand your history, movement patterns, and underlying causes — not just your current complaint.

Manual Therapy

Hands-on treatment to restore mobility, reduce pain, and improve tissue health — tailored to your specific needs.

Movement Re-Education

Correcting faulty movement patterns that cause pain or inefficiency, and building lasting new habits through guided exercise.

Injury Rehabilitation

Structured recovery programs following surgery, sports injuries, or accidents — from acute care through return to full activity.

Pregnancy & Postnatal Care

Specialized treatment for women during and post pregnancy — safe, evidence-based support for every stage of the journey.

Performance Optimization

For athletes and active individuals seeking to train smarter, prevent injury, and reach their peak potential safely.
